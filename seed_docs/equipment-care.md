# Equipment Care

How we keep the machines in our cafés running. Ask the shift lead before doing anything not on this page.

## Espresso machines

Backflush with detergent every night at close (see the barista handbook). Change the group gaskets and shower screens every 6 months; the date of the last change is on the sticker under the drip tray.

Descale is handled by the water filter: replace the filter cartridge every 4 months or after 3,000 litres, whichever comes first. The filter counter is on the display under Settings › Water.

## Grinders

Clean the burrs with grinder cleaning tablets every Monday: run 15 g of tablets through, then 30 g of coffee, and throw both away.

Replace the burrs every 800 kg of coffee for the house espresso grinder and every 1,200 kg for the decaf grinder. The counter is on the grinder display.

## When something breaks

1. Put an "out of order" card on it.
2. Log it in the maintenance channel with a photo and what happened.
3. For anything involving water leaks or electrics, switch it off at the wall and call the technician on the card by the machine. Don't open the machine yourself.

Our technician contract covers a visit within 24 hours on weekdays. Weekend call-outs cost €180 and need the café manager's approval.
