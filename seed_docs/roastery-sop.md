# Roastery Standard Operating Procedures

Northwind Coffee Co. roastery, Calle Portales 40, Logroño.

## Roast schedule

We roast on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Café orders placed by 14:00 the day before are roasted the next roast day and delivered within 48 hours of roasting.

Online orders ship the day after they are roasted. We never ship coffee older than 10 days from roast to retail customers.

## Roast profiles

| Coffee | Charge temp | First crack | Drop | Development |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Northwind Blend | 205 °C | ~8:30 | 11:00 | 20% |
| Ethiopia Guji (filter) | 200 °C | ~8:00 | 9:45 | 17% |
| Colombia Huila | 205 °C | ~8:15 | 10:30 | 19% |
| Decaf (Swiss Water) | 195 °C | ~8:45 | 11:15 | 21% |

Decaf always roasts last of the day, after a full chaff clean, so no regular coffee mixes into it.

## Quality control

Cup every batch the day after roasting. Score with the SCA form. Any batch below 82 points is not sold as single origin; it goes into the Northwind Blend if it scores 80 or more, otherwise it is used for staff coffee.

Keep a 250 g retained sample of every batch for 3 months, labelled with the batch number.

## Safety

The roaster is never left unattended while it is on. Empty the chaff collector after every 5 batches and at the end of the day: chaff fires are the most common roastery fire.

There is a CO2 extinguisher next to the roaster and a fire blanket by the door. If smoke comes out of the drum door, close the air damper, switch off the burner and keep the drum turning. Never open the drum door during a fire.

## Green coffee storage

Green coffee is stored in GrainPro bags on pallets, at 18–22 °C and 50–60% humidity. First in, first out. Any green coffee older than 12 months from harvest is cupped before use.
