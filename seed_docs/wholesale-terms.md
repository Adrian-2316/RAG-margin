# Wholesale Terms

For cafés, restaurants and offices buying Northwind coffee in bulk. Last updated 1 September 2026.

## Ordering

The minimum order is 5 kg per delivery. Orders placed by 14:00 are roasted on the next roast day (Monday, Wednesday or Friday) and delivered within 48 hours of roasting.

Delivery is free within La Rioja and Navarra for orders of 10 kg or more. Below 10 kg, delivery costs €12. Outside those regions we ship by courier at cost.

## Prices

Wholesale prices are on the current price list. Prices are fixed for 3 months at a time and we give 30 days' notice of any change.

Volume discount: 5% off for 20 kg or more per month, 8% off for 40 kg or more per month, calculated on the previous month's volume.

## Payment

New accounts pay on order for the first 3 months. After that, payment terms are 30 days from invoice date by bank transfer. Invoices more than 15 days overdue pause new deliveries until they are paid.

## Equipment loan

Accounts buying 15 kg or more a month can borrow a two-group espresso machine and a grinder at no charge. We service them twice a year. If volume drops below 15 kg for three months in a row, we ask for €90 a month rental or the equipment back.

## Training

Every new wholesale account gets one free barista training session (3 hours) for up to four staff, at our roastery or at their café.

## Quality issues

If a bag arrives damaged or the coffee tastes wrong, tell us within 7 days of delivery with the batch number from the bag. We replace it with the next delivery, no questions asked.
