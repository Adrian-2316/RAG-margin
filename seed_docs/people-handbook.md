# People Handbook

Northwind Coffee Co. · version 4.2 · applies to everyone on a contract with us, in the cafés, the roastery and the office.

## Working hours

A full-time week is 38 hours. Café rotas are published every Thursday for the following two weeks. Swaps are fine as long as both people agree and the shift lead approves it in the rota app before the shift starts.

Breaks: 30 minutes paid for any shift longer than 6 hours, plus a free drink of your choice every shift.

## Holidays

Full-time staff get 25 days of paid holiday a year plus public holidays. Part-time staff get the same, pro rata to their contracted hours.

You can carry over up to 5 unused days into the first quarter of the next year. Anything above 5 days is lost on 31 December unless your manager agreed in writing that work stopped you from taking it.

Request holiday in the rota app at least 4 weeks ahead for anything longer than 3 days. Summer weeks (July and August) are allocated in March, first come first served, with a maximum of two weeks in a row per person.

## Sick leave

If you're ill, message your shift lead before your shift starts, or at least 2 hours before for an opening shift. A phone call is better than a text for opening shifts.

The first 3 days of sickness need no doctor's note. From day 4 you need a note from a doctor. We pay full salary for up to 20 sick days a year; after that, statutory sick pay applies.

## Remote work

Office roles can work from home up to 3 days a week. Café and roastery roles can't, for obvious reasons. Everyone in the office is expected in on Tuesdays for the weekly planning meeting.

## Learning budget

Every employee has €600 a year for training, books or courses. SCA coffee courses are always approved. Anything else needs a two-line note to your manager on how it helps your work. Unused budget does not carry over.

## Staff discount

40% off beans and merch in our cafés and online shop (code given on your first day), and 20% off equipment. The discount is for you and your household, not for resale.
