# Barista Handbook

Northwind Coffee Co. · café standards · read this before your first shift.

## Espresso recipe

Our house espresso is the Northwind Blend. The recipe is 18 g of coffee in, 38 g of espresso out, in 27 to 31 seconds. Brew temperature is 93 °C.

Dial in every morning before opening and again after lunch: pull a shot, taste it, and adjust the grind before changing anything else. If the shot runs faster than 25 seconds, grind finer. Slower than 33 seconds, grind coarser. Log every adjustment on the dial-in sheet by the grinder.

Single-origin espresso uses 18 g in, 40 g out, in 26 to 30 seconds, unless the guest-coffee card says otherwise.

## Milk

Steam whole milk to 60–65 °C. Never go above 70 °C: the milk scalds and loses its sweetness. Oat milk is steamed to 55–60 °C.

Purge the steam wand before and after every jug and wipe it with the blue cloth, never the yellow one (yellow is for the bar top).

Milk jugs are emptied after every drink. We never re-steam milk.

## Allergens

Every drink with nuts (hazelnut syrup, almond milk) is made with a separate jug marked with a red band. If a guest tells you about a nut allergy, tell the shift lead and use the red-band-free station.

We can never promise a product is allergen-free. Say "we take care, but our kitchen handles nuts, gluten and milk", and show the allergen folder by the till.

## Opening checklist

1. Switch on the espresso machines at least 30 minutes before opening.
2. Flush each group head for 5 seconds.
3. Dial in the house espresso (see above).
4. Fill the milk fridge and check dates. Anything that expires today goes at the front.
5. Count the float: €150 in the till, in the denominations on the float card.

## Closing checklist

1. Backflush each group with detergent, then twice with clean water.
2. Soak portafilter baskets and steam wand tips in cleaner for 20 minutes.
3. Empty the grinder hoppers into airtight tubs. Never leave beans in the hopper overnight.
4. Cash up: count the till, keep the €150 float, and put the rest in the safe with the Z-report.
